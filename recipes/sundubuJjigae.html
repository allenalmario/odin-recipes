Sundubu Jjigae

Home Page

Sundubu Jjigae is a Korean stew made with soft (uncurdled) tofu as a highlight ingredient.

Ingredients

  • Silken Tofu - 1 block
  • Onion - 1 medium
  • Garlic - 3 to 4 cloves
  • Green Onion - 2 stalks
  • Kimchi - 1/2 cup
  • Sesame Oil - 1 table spoon
  • Any cut of tender steak like skirt steak or flat iron steak - 6 to 7 oz
  • Gochugaru coarse flakes - 1 to 2 tablespoons
  • Gochujang - 1 tablespoon
  • Fish sauce - 1 tablespoon
  • Soy Sauce - 1 tablespoon
  • Beef or Anchovie stock or water - 2 cups
  • Optional: Salt and Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cut the silken tofu into cubes and set aside.
  2. Slice the onion, mince the garlic, chop the green onions, and cut the kimchi into small pieces.
  3. Thinly slice the steak against the grain.
  4. In a ttukbbaegi or medium pot, heat 1 tablespoon of sesame oil over medium-high heat.
  5. Add the sliced beef and sauté for 2-3 minutes until it begins to brown. If you want, you can season the beef lightly with a pinch of salt and pepper. Remove the beef from the pot and set it aside.
  6. In the same pot, add the minced garlic and sliced onion. Cook for 2-3 minutes until fragrant and softened.
  7. Add the chopped kimchi to the pot and cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Stir in gochugaru (Korean chili flakes) and gochujang (chili paste), mixing well to coat the ingredients.
  9. Pour in the beef or anchovy stock (or water) and bring the mixture to a simmer. Let it cook for about 5 minutes to allow the flavors to blend.
  10. Gently add the silken tofu cubes into the pot. You can break the tofu into smaller pieces if you prefer, or leave them whole for a more rustic texture.
  11. Return the cooked beef to the pot and stir gently.
  12. Add 1 tablespoon of soy sauce and 1 tablespoon of fish sauce. Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed, adding more soy sauce, fish sauce, or gochugaru if you want more flavor or spice.
  13. Allow the stew to simmer for about 5-10 minutes so the tofu absorbs the flavors and the soup becomes flavorful. You can taste and adjust seasoning if necessary.
  14. Put the sundubu jjigae into bowls. Garnish with the chopped green onions on top.
