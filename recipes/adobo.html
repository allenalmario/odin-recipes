Chicken Adobo

Chicken Adobo is a classic Filipino dish, known for its savory, tangy, and slightly sweet flavors.

Ingredients

    • Chicken pieces (thighs, drumsticks, or a mix) - 2.2 lbs
    • Onion - 1 medium
    • Garlic - 5 to 6 cloves
    • Bay Leaves - 2
    • Pepper - replaces pepper corns
    • Soy Sauce - 1/2 cup
    • Vinegar - 1/2 cup
    • Water - 1/2 cup
    • Brownsugar - 1 tablespoon
    • Oil - 2 tablespoons
    • Salt and Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the chicken, soy sauce, garlic, onion, bay leaves, peppercorns, and vinegar and mix to marinate for 30 minutes to 1 hour.
  2. Add the marinated chicken (with the marinade) to the pot. Sauté the chicken for about 5-7 minutes, until it starts to brown slightly.
  3. Pour in the water, and add the sugar if you're using it. Stir to combine everything.
  4. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and cover the pot. Let it simmer for about 30-40 minutes, or until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
  5. Occasionally, check the pot and stir to ensure the chicken is evenly cooked.
  6. Once the chicken is tender, uncover the pot and let it simmer for another 10-15 minutes to reduce the sauce and intensify the flavors.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Adjust the balance of vinegar and soy sauce to suit your preference (add more vinegar for tang or more soy sauce for saltiness).
  8. Serve the Chicken Adobo hot over steamed rice.
